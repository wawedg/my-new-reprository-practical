# FocusTime User Manual

A short guide to using FocusTime. To open the app, see the
[README](../README.md#how-to-start-the-app).

## Using the timer

1. Press **Start** to begin a focus session. Press it again to pause.
2. Press **Reset** to start the current session over.
3. Use the **Focus**, **Short Break** and **Long Break** buttons at the top
   to switch sessions by hand.
4. When time is up, you'll hear a sound. The next session is ready to start.

The line under the clock (for example, "Pomodoro 1 of 4") shows how close you
are to a long break.

The first time you press Start, your browser may ask to show notifications.
Choose **Allow** to get pop-up alerts.

## Managing tasks

- **Add a task:** type it in the box and press **Add** (or Enter).
- **Work on a task:** press **Focus** next to it. The timer starts and the task
  appears under the clock. Press it again to stop.
- **Finish a task:** tick the box next to it. It moves to **Completed**.
- **Rename a task:** press ✎ or double-click the task name. (Not possible while
  the timer is running.)
- **Delete a task:** press 🗑.
- **Search:** type a word in the box and press **Search**. Press **Clear** to
  see all tasks again.
- **Sort:** use the **Sort** menu to order tasks by date added or A–Z.

## Changing the background

1. Press the **Background** button.
2. Pick a color under **Solid**, or a photo under **Unsplash**.
3. Press **Reset to default** to go back to the original look.

## Good to know

- Your tasks are saved in your browser. Using a different browser or clearing
  your browsing data will remove them.
- If you close the page while the timer runs, it keeps counting and catches up
  when you come back.
- Want to learn more about the method? Open **About Pomodoro** at the top of
  the page.
